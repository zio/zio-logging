IO Logging

The mark is a Z set as five log lines. The middle line, the one you search for, is amber. Everything else on this sheet follows from that.

01

Mark

grid 24 · line 4 · gap 1 · step 7

Five lines, 4 units tall and 1 unit apart, on a 24-unit square. The top and bottom lines run the full width. The three middle lines are 10 units long and step 7 units from right to left: the diagonal of the Z.

One accent element: the middle line. It is amber in every version that has colour; every other line takes the ink colour. One-colour versions keep the line, in ink.

The gaps are part of the mark. They are 1 unit wide, which is 1 px at 24 px. Below 24 px use the small cut (section 03), never a scaled-down master.

The mark is the Z of “ZIO” (family rule: a Z mark sits inline as the first letter). It is never set beside a full “ZIO Logging”, which would say Z twice.

Mark height = cap height of Geist SemiBold. Space from mark to “I” = one twelfth of the mark height. Tracking −2%. Horizontal is the default; stacked is for square spaces.

Smallest lockup: mark 16 px tall (type about 23 px). Smaller than that, use the mark alone.

Clear space: one line height on every side, 4/24 of the mark height (8 px around a 48 px mark). It scales with the mark. Nothing enters it: no text, edges, rules or other logos.

Small cut, 16 to 23 px. Drawn on a 16-unit grid: lines 3, 2, 2, 2, 3 px with 1 px gaps, so all five lines survive. Best at exactly 16 or 32 px.

Master, 24 px and up. Crisp at 24, 48, 72, 96 px (whole pixels per unit). The mark alone is never smaller than 16 px.

06

Type

Geist · 400, 600 · headings, body, wordmark
Logs that keep their context

ZIO Logging is simple logging for ZIO apps, with correlation, context, and pluggable backends out of the box with integrations for common logging backends.

Geist Mono · 400, 500 · code, log output, labels
ZIO.logInfo("order placed") ZIO.logWarning("retrying payment")
level=INFO message="order placed" level=WARN message="retrying payment"

One sans, one mono, no third family. Both are open source on Google Fonts.

Sentence case everywhere. Caps only for mono labels: 12 to 13 px, weight 500, tracking +8%.

Headings tighten as they grow: −2% at 48 px and up, −1% at 32 px, 0 at 24 px and below. Body 16 px, line height 1.55, at most 72 characters a line.

07

Social cards

1200 × 630 for X, Slack, Discord, Facebook and LinkedIn; 1280 × 640 for GitHub. Each in light and dark; the light card is the fallback when a platform shows one.

Safe zone 7.5% of width: 90 px on 1200, 96 px on 1280. All text and the lockup sit inside it.

The mark bleeds off the right edge as a tone-on-tone ghost: #EEEEEA on White (1.16:1), #20232B on Terminal (1.18:1). Upload as PNG; keep SVG as the source.

08

Don’t

Close the gaps. Without them it is a Z, not a log.

Colour any line but the middle one, or more than one.

Stretch, squash or rotate the mark.

Use bright amber on a light ground (2.04:1). Light grounds take Deep amber.

ZIO Logging

Set the mark beside “ZIO Logging”. That says Z twice; the mark is the Z.

Also never: gradients, glows, shadows, strokes or 3D on the mark; the mark on a busy photo or pattern.
