Four concepts

Each mark says one thing about logging. Amber is the accent: the WARN colour, and the colour of old terminal text. Pick one or two for round 2.

01 · Record

A level stripe and three message lines: one log record, as a log viewer draws it. Not a Z; sits beside the name.

02 · Tagged Z · recommended

The level stripe in front of a solid Z. In the lockup the stripe stands before “ZIO Logging”, so the name reads as a log record.

IO Logging

16 px: clearest of the four. Accent is a separate bar, not part of the Z (Prelude) or a cut (Blocks).

03 · Line Z

A Z set as five log lines. The middle line, the one you grep for, is amber.

04 · Tail

A solid Z and a block cursor: the log is still being written (tail -f). In the lockup the cursor moves to the end of the name.
