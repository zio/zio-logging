## Tried and dropped on the grid

- Timestamp gutter (a column of amber stubs before each line): it turns back into 2D Tagged lines.
- Stack trace as many thin indented lines: too fine at 16 px, and only a variant of 2A.
- River with square gaps (first try): read as a brick wall. Slanted gap ends, as in 2B, fixed it.

## What I saw at 16, 24, 32 and 96 px (light and dark)

- **Tail**: reads like 2A; the top line is 1 px at 16 px, still visible. The weight change gives it direction.
- **River**: from 24 px up, a crisp white Z through the lines. At 16 px it reads as a slash through lines; the Z is there but faint. Risk: a field with a shape cut out is the Blocks way of working.
- **Prompt**: a clean `>` at every size. Risk: a chevron is close to HTTP's arrows.
- **Log L**: at 16 px it reads as an "align left" text icon more than an L. The inline lockup is what sells it.

## Recommendation

**3B River.** It is the most original of the five and the most specific to logging: the Z is made by the spacing of text, which no other family mark does. It is one colour, so the mark needs no accent rule. If Milad wants the safer path, **3A Tail** is the closest to 2A and reads best at 16 px.

Clear-space unit for River: one line height (4 of 24 units, one sixth of the mark height) on every side. For Tail: the middle line's height (4 of 24), the same value.
